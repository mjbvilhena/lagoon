# Lagoon

Lagoon is a private knowledge vault for Miguel's Android phone. It is for him alone. He keeps the links, notes, images, and PDFs he wants to find again in one place on the device.

He comes across those things in other apps and has nowhere private that holds them together. Lagoon is that place. He can search it without a network, and a private copy can follow him to another install.

## What it does

- From the share sheet, he can save a link, a piece of text, an image, or a PDF.
- He can search the full text offline, including text recognized on the phone from images and scanned PDFs.
- He can tag items. A cloud AI can suggest topics from their content.
- He can set a reminder or an expiry date on an item.
- The vault is encrypted on the phone. When he is online, that encrypted copy syncs to his private Google Drive space on its own.
- Items he marks as sensitive ask for a biometric before they open.
- He can use a light theme, a dark theme, or follow the system setting.

## Where it stands

The product specification and the epics are approved. The app is not implemented yet.
