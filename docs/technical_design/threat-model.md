## System under review

- Trust boundaries, from the stories and the Proposed ADRs, not from a running app: the phone holds the vault and the Keystore wrap; Drive `appDataFolder` holds ciphertext and a clear KDF header; the xAI Grok API at `api.x.ai` would receive ordinary text if that provider stays the recommendation (ADR-0003); the Cursor API is not a text-completion call; ML Kit would receive metrics after acceptance (ADR-0004); another app can send a share or receive one item; link preview is an outbound HTTPS fetch. PDF renderers run in an isolated process (ADR-0005).
- Actor: Miguel. A second person is not named. The interesting other parties are whoever holds the phone, whoever can read Drive app data, the topic provider, and an app that sends or receives a share.
- Assets: passphrase, vault key, wrap blob, item bodies, attachments, tokens in URLs, OAuth tokens, the topic API key, notification text.
- Out of scope: exploit steps, Play Store distribution, E10 trips. Alarms stay inexact (US-77 is Draft; the specification already requires inexact alarms). The cipher and container are in scope as ADR-0006, which is Proposed and not accepted.

There is no running app. Rows are hypotheses about the written design, except T-2 and T-26, which are accepted risks of decided rules. Evidence is a story or an ADR, not a trace.

No cookies, Referer, or authorization. User-Agent `LagoonPreview`. At most 5 redirects, 15 seconds, 10 MiB decompressed | Hypothesis |
| T-9 | Spoofing | Sideloaded update | US-81 | A different signature replaces the app, or uninstall is treated as an upgrade and the key copy is gone | Same application id, same release key, higher `versionCode`. Tap opens Lagoon and the item waits for US-37. Ordinary reminders may show the title. That ordinary text is an accepted residual. Which install posts is US-55 | Hypothesis |
| T-11 | Elevation of privilege | Passphrase reset | US-35, US-37, US-86, ADR-0001 | Someone who does not hold the key, or who cancels the unlock, sets a new passphrase | A phone with no key copy cannot reset. "I've forgotten my passphrase" there only offers a new empty vault after an explicit warning, and does not overwrite Drive until he confirms. A phone that holds the key resets only after a successful US-37 unlock. Cancel or fail of that unlock changes nothing. The reset accepts the US-37 biometric or device PIN. The 24-hour wait and the notice are the mitigation for a thief who knows the PIN (T-25). Re-encrypt mints a new salt. On a normal change the other install must not overwrite the new Drive copy. On a forgotten-passphrase reset the Drive copy keeps the old passphrase for 24 hours, another phone that holds the key can cancel, and no other install overwrites the Drive copy (T-25) | Hypothesis |
| T-12 | Information disclosure | Topic request | US-41, US-75, US-79, ADR-0003 | Vault text is used to train a model, or a sensitive item or an image is included, or the API key ships in the APK or the URL | The current recommendation is the xAI Grok API. Vault text is not sent unless `x-zero-data-retention` was `true`. 100 calls per install per month. A second phone skips a call when provenance already has the same item id and field hash. The paid Gemini API was ruled out | Hypothesis |
| T-13 | Information disclosure | ML Kit metrics | US-07, US-31, ADR-0004 | OCR starts ML Kit before he accepts, or a second initialize in the same process ignores a withdrawal | Bundled model only. Datatransport components are named on the US-80 allowlist or removed. Image and recognized text stay on device per the terms the research note cites. Metrics themselves still leave after acceptance (T-17) | Hypothesis |
| T-14 | Denial of service | Passphrase guesses on the phone, and preview size | US-35, US-79, ADR-0001 | A tight loop runs Argon2id on the phone, or a preview download fills the disk | Retry wait 2s doubling to 32s, then each further wrong attempt waits 32s. No permanent lockout. The KDF does not run during the wait. A correct passphrase clears the delay. A passphrase of fewer than 12 characters is refused, and a passphrase on the bundled common-password list is refused, before the KDF. There is no strength meter. Preview stops at 15 seconds and 10 MiB. This row is the on-phone delay. Offline guessing is T-19 | Hypothesis |

The global endpoint does not guarantee a region. The Cursor API overview says the Cloud Agents API is not a chat-completions API, so it is not this call. Cloud-agent prompts are kept indefinitely by default even though Privacy Mode says Cursor does not train on them | Hypothesis |

| T-24 | Information disclosure | Compromised Google account | US-36, US-78, ADR-0001 | Whoever holds the account reads app data, deletes it, or uploads a replacement | They see ciphertext and the clear header, not the key and not the wrap blob. | T-25 | Elevation of privilege | Stolen phone with a known PIN | US-37, US-75, US-86, ADR-0001 | Sensitive items open, and the passphrase is reset, because the PIN is known | The reset accepts the US-37 biometric or device PIN. That factor is decided. The mitigation for a thief who knows the PIN is the 24-hour wait and the notice, not a stricter factor. A known PIN still opens sensitive items on that phone. That opening is the US-37 residual. The Drive copy keeps the old passphrase for 24 hours after a pending-reset marker is on Drive. During those 24 hours every other phone that holds the key shows a notice and can cancel. The notice has no passphrase and no item content. A normal change, where he enters the current passphrase, does not wait. One phone has no other install to show the notice, and the Drive copy still waits. If the resetting phone is offline before the marker is uploaded, the clock has not started. A cancel leaves Drive on the old passphrase. Items saved on the resetting phone during the 24 hours are copied into the restored vault under the old passphrase and are not lost. During the wait the phone vault is already under the new passphrase. That local copy, and a sole phone, are the residual. The phone vault and the Drive copy each stay under exactly one passphrase | Hypothesis |
| T-26 | Tampering | Sensitive mark lost in a merge | US-75, ADR-0002 | Accepted risk. The mark merges like any other field: the later `updatedAt` wins, and a tie goes to the lower install id. No extra control | Accepted risk |

When the 30 days end, the tombstone spreads. The tombstone is then kept until every known install has merged it and 90 days have passed since the tombstone. After that garbage collection, an old zip can bring the item back. Equal timestamps use the lower install id. A wrong clock can still make an older edit look newer. That residual is named. A reminder is a local time, so 09:00 fires at 09:00 in the zone the phone is in (US-55). A new phone re-creates the key from the passphrase. Topic calls have no residency promise (T-16) | Hypothesis |

On that reset the Drive copy keeps the old passphrase for 24 hours (T-25). This row does not add a recovery copy of the key | Hypothesis |

## Missing controls (defensive)

Unchecked because there is no code. The design names the control. It does not demonstrate it.

- [ ] Authentication / session. US-37 covers sensitive items. The vault key does not use user authentication, by US-35. Passphrase reset is T-11 and T-25. The forgotten-passphrase reset accepts the US-37 biometric or device PIN, leaves Drive on the old passphrase for 24 hours, and another phone that holds the key can cancel.
- [ ] Authorization checks on the new path. Share-out of a sensitive item waits on US-37 (US-64). No second user exists to authorize.
- [ ] Input validation and output encoding as appropriate. PDF handling (ADR-0005), preview host rules (US-79), topic payload allowlist (ADR-0003), zip path and size caps (ADR-0006), share URI scheme and image cap (data layer).
- [ ] Encryption in transit (and at rest if the change stores sensitive data). At rest is T-1 and ADR-0006. Preview and topic calls are HTTPS.
- [ ] Audit logging of sensitive actions (no secrets in logs). T-15 and T-31. No new analytics pipeline.
- [ ] Rate / size limits. T-14, T-19, T-21, T-27, ADR-0003's 100000-character send bound, US-31's OCR page cap.
- [ ] Supply-chain controls in T-30. Not configured. The workflow is tag-pinned. There is no Gradle build yet.

## Secrets handling

No credential value is written in this model. US-26 and US-41 already say the repo and the APK do not carry a client secret or a plaintext topic key. ADR-0001 forbids the vault key on Drive. ADR-0003 stores the topic key only as vault ciphertext on that install and sends it in a header. ADR-0002 does not sync the key.

## Residual risk

A lost passphrase on a phone that no longer holds the key still loses the content (US-35, T-29). Old Drive revisions remain decryptable with the old passphrase until Drive purges them, and a zip he keeps remains decryptable after that (T-5). Ordinary notification titles are visible, including to a watch, a car, or a notification listener, and an ordinary screen can appear in Recents (T-10, T-32). The topic provider is Decision pending between the xAI Grok API and the Cursor API. The recommendation is Grok, and only after `x-zero-data-retention` is `true`. Without that setting the security FAQ says prompts are stored for 30 days. There is no documented EU endpoint (T-16). The Cursor API does not offer a text completion. The paid Gemini API was ruled out. ML Kit metrics continue after he accepts OCR (T-17). Image decoding and SQLCipher stay in the app process (T-22). A brand-new phone has no generation mark yet (T-24). A later edit on another phone can clear the sensitive mark. That is an accepted risk, and the mitigation is the documented merge, with no extra control (T-26). A forgotten-passphrase reset accepts the US-37 biometric or device PIN. The Drive copy stays on the old passphrase for 24 hours, and another phone that holds the key can cancel (T-25). That wait and that notice are the mitigation for a thief who knows the PIN. A known PIN still opens sensitive items on the stolen phone. During that wait the resetting phone is already under the new passphrase, and a sole phone has nobody to cancel. The PDF definition is not decided. A phone without secure hardware can hold a vault after he accepts the warning, and someone with that phone could extract the key. That is an accepted risk (T-2). A 12-character passphrase can still be guessed offline (T-19). A phone that cannot meet the memory floor cannot set up a vault. This model does not claim the product is secure or STRIDE-compliant.

## Anti-patterns

- Exploit steps, payloads, or a procedure for breaking the KDF.
- A "secure" or "STRIDE-compliant" claim.
- Inventing a CVE number. The API 27 renderer bug is cited by its public issuetracker id in ADR-0005, not as a new CVE.
- Marking a hypothesis as something observed in an app. There is no app.
- Treating a Decision pending option as the mitigation that was chosen.
